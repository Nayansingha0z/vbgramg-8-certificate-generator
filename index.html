⭐ 0. Advanced PDF Text Editor — Search, Replace & OCR (v12 PDF compatibility fix)

Works locally in this browser after libraries load. Includes a second-pass PDF compatibility/repair attempt. The original is never changed; edits export as a new PDF. Best for ordinary text-based PDFs. Complex layouts, tables, embedded fonts and scanned pages may not preserve exact formatting.

Important: replacement overlays the matched text item; it does not rewrite the original PDF text stream. A search match inside a text item may cover the whole item. Use this for routine corrections only, not legal redaction or confidential-data removal.
